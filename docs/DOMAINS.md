# Domains

This file enumerates the context modules and schemas found under `lib/medcamp`. Public functions generally follow Phoenix context conventions: `list_*`, `filter_*`, `count_*`, `get_*!`, `create_*`, `update_*`, `delete_*`, and `change_*`. Domain-specific functions are noted where they drive important workflows.

| Domain | Responsibility | Schemas / tables | Notable functions |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `Medcamp.Accounts` | Users, roles, session tokens, password flows, PIN lookup, login timestamps. | `Accounts.User` (`users`), `Accounts.UserToken` (`users_tokens`) | `register_user/1`, `get_user_by_email/1`, `get_admin_by_otp/1`, `get_support_staff_by_otp/1`, `generate_user_session_token/1`, `update_user_last_logged_in/1` |
| `Medcamp.Patients` | Patient demographics, GSRN/PIN handling, medical camp patient flags. | `Patients.Patient` (`patients`) | `get_available_gsrn/0`, patient list/filter/create/update helpers |
| `Medcamp.PatientVisits` | Outpatient/inpatient visit records and payment status. | `PatientVisits.PatientVisit` (`patient_visits`) | create/list/filter visit helpers, doctor/patient visit queries |
| `Medcamp.DoctorNotes` | Doctor consultations, diagnosis, investigations, treatment, admission links. | `DoctorNotes.DoctorNote` (`doctor_notes`) | doctor note CRUD and patient/note queries |
| `Medcamp.NurseNotes`, `Medcamp.NurseProcedures`, `Medcamp.Triages` | Nursing notes, procedures, room/triage workflow support. | `nurse_notes`, `nurse_procedures`, `triages` | nursing CRUD, patient-specific queries, payment triggers through LiveViews |
| `Medcamp.Inpatient`, `Medcamp.CadexNotes`, `Medcamp.AdmissionRequests` | Admission requests and inpatient documentation. | `admission_requests`, `admission_request_line_items`, `admission_notes`, `continuation_notes`, `treatment_sheets`, `vital_records`, `discharge_summaries`, `cadex_notes` | admission, line-item, vitals, treatment, discharge, and cadex helpers |
| `Medcamp.LabTests`, `Medcamp.LabResults`, `Medcamp.LabTestTemplates` | Lab catalog, lab orders/results, templated test entry, interpretation payloads. | `lab_tests`, `lab_results`, embedded lab result tests, `lab_test_categories`, `lab_test_templates`, `lab_test_entries` | `create_lab_result/1`, `create_camp_lab_order/1`, template/category CRUD, interpretation refresh helpers |
| `Medcamp.RadiologyTests`, `Medcamp.RadiologyResults` | Radiology catalog and ordered imaging results. | `radiology_tests`, `radiology_results`, embedded scans | radiology test/result CRUD and patient/doctor queries |
| `Medcamp.Procedures`, `Medcamp.DoctorProcedures`, `Medcamp.SubsidizedProcedures` | Clinical procedure catalog and performed doctor procedures. | `procedure`, `doctor_procedures`, `subsidized_procedures` | procedure CRUD and billable procedure helpers |
| `Medcamp.Drugs`, `Medcamp.DrugBatches`, `Medcamp.DrugAllocations`, `Medcamp.DrugsGiven`, `Medcamp.PharmacyLogs`, `Medcamp.DangerousDrugRegisters` | Pharmacy catalog, active batches, prescriptions, dispensing, pharmacy audit logs, controlled-drug register. | `drugs`, `drug_batches`, `drug_allocations`, `drugs_given`, `pharmacy_logs`, `dangerous_drug_registers` | active-batch queries, allocation/given-drug CRUD, logs/register helpers |
| `Medcamp.InventoriesReceived`, `Medcamp.Batches`, `Medcamp.InventoriesIssues`, `Medcamp.Inventories`, `Medcamp.StockTakes`, `Medcamp.StockAlerts`, `Medcamp.ConsumptionAnalysis` | Inventory master items, received batches, issues, general inventory, stock takes, low-stock alerts, consumption reporting. | `inventories_received`, `batches`, `inventories_issued`, `general_inventory_items`, `general_inventory_transactions`, `stock_takes`, `stock_take_entries` | batch lookup by GTIN/batch, in-store lists, stock alert helpers, consumption queries |
| `Medcamp.Suppliers` | Legacy supplier management plus supplier documents, invoices, quotes, delivery notes, ASNs, recalls. | `suppliers`, `supplier_documents`, `supplier_invoices`, `supplier_quotes`, `supplier_delivery_notes`, `supplier_advance_ship_notices`, `supplier_recalls` | supplier CRUD, supplier portal document/order helpers, low-stock supplier queries |
| `Medcamp.Procurement.*` | Modern procurement portal: supplier onboarding, RFQs, quote comparison, proformas, POs, invoices, shipments, GRNs, notifications. | `rfqs`, `rfq_items`, `rfq_invitations`, `quotes`, `quote_items`, `proforma_invoices`, `proforma_invoice_items`, `purchase_orders`, `purchase_order_items`, `invoices`, `invoice_items`, `shipment_advices`, `shipment_items`, `goods_received_notes`, `grn_items`, `procurement_notifications`, `supplier_directors` | `Rfqs`, `Quotes`, `ProformaInvoices`, `PurchaseOrders`, `Invoices`, `Shipments`, `GoodsReceived`, `Suppliers`, `Notifications`, `Dashboard`, `References` |
| `Medcamp.Rooms`, `Medcamp.RoomAllocations`, `Medcamp.RoomEquipments` | Rooms/beds, allocation to patients, and equipment. | `rooms`, `room_allocations`, `room_equipments` | room CRUD, allocation CRUD, GLN lookup support |
| `Medcamp.Departments`, `Medcamp.SOPs`, `Medcamp.Requisitions` | Departments, SOP documents, and internal stock/service requisitions. | `departments`, `sops`, `requisitions` | department/SOP/requisition CRUD and role-scoped requisition screens |
| `Medcamp.Appointments`, `Medcamp.Referrals`, `Medcamp.PatientFormRecords` | Appointments, doctor referrals, reusable clinical forms. | `appointments`, public booking data, `referrals`, `patient_form_records` | public appointment creation, patient appointment/referral/form queries |
| `Medcamp.Mch` | Mother and child health. | `mch_mothers`, `mch_pregnancies`, `mch_children`, `mch_antenatal_profiles`, `mch_physical_examinations`, `mch_anc_visits`, `mch_td_vaccinations`, `mch_malaria_prophylaxis`, `mch_ifas_supplements`, `mch_deworming_maternal`, `mch_deliveries`, `mch_pnc_mother_visits`, `mch_pnc_baby_visits`, `mch_growth_measurements`, `mch_immunizations`, `mch_vitamin_a_supplements`, `mch_child_deworming`, `mch_developmental_milestones`, `mch_eye_assessments` | MCH record CRUD and patient-linked mother/child flows |
| `Medcamp.WalletDeposits`, `Medcamp.WalletWithdrawals`, `Medcamp.Mpesas`, `Medcamp.Pay`, `Medcamp.PatientCharges` | Patient wallet, M-Pesa callbacks, payments, and charge batches. | `wallet_deposits`, `wallet_withdrawals`, `mpesas`, `patient_charges`, `patient_charge_batches` | payment creation/update, wallet usage/statistics, M-Pesa transaction handling |
| `Medcamp.DailyActivities`, `Medcamp.StaffMeals`, `Medcamp.ShiftHandovers`, `Medcamp.Todos`, `Medcamp.Visitors` | Support staff operations, meal supply entries, shift handover, todos, visitor book. | `daily_activities`, `staff_meal_supplies`, `shift_handovers`, `todos`, `visitor_book_entries` | daily activity history, meal PIN flow support, handover/todo CRUD |
| `Medcamp.Blogs`, `Medcamp.Feedback`, `Medcamp.CommunityHealthSurveys`, `Medcamp.MedicalCampReports` | Public content, patient feedback, survey intake, medical camp reports. | `blog_posts`, `blog_sections`, `patient_feedbacks`, `community_health_survey_responses` | blog default posts, survey create/list, feedback CRUD, camp reporting |
| `Medcamp.AuditLogs`, `Medcamp.UserLoginSessions`, `Medcamp.QualityAssurance` | Audit trail, login session history, lab quality assurance charts. | `audit_logs`, `user_login_sessions`, `quality_assurance_charts` | audited Repo operations, login/logout recording, QA chart helpers |
| Utility modules | Calculators, GS1/GTIN parsing, notification, email, AI interpretation, pagination, scheduler, seed imports. | No primary tables or helper-only schemas | `Medcamp.BmiCalculator`, `ObstetricCalculator`, `DataMatrixParser`, `Gs1Decoder`, `Sendgrid`, `Notify`, `ArtificialIntelligence.*`, `Pagination`, `Scheduler` |
