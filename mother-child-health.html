Feeding Recommendations

0-6 Months

Exclusive Breastfeeding

  • Breastfeed immediately within 1 hour after birth
  • Give colostrum (first yellowish milk)
  • Breastfeed day and night, at least 8 times in 24 hours
  • No other foods or fluids needed
  • If LBW, feed every 2-3 hours
Exclusive breastfeeding protects against HIV better than mixed feeding

6-9 Months

Breastfeeding + Complementary Foods

  • Continue breastfeeding as often as child wants
  • Give thick porridge or well-mashed foods
  • Include animal-source foods and vitamin A-rich fruits/vegetables
  • Start with 2-3 tablespoons, increase to 1/2 cup
  • 2-3 meals per day + 1-2 snacks

9-12 Months

Family Foods + Breastfeeding

  • Mashed or finely chopped family foods
  • 1/2 cup at each meal (cup = 250ml)
  • 3-4 meals per day + 1-2 snacks
  • Give small chewable items as snacks
  • Let child try to eat, provide help if needed

12-24 Months

Family Foods + Breastfeeding

  • 3/4 cup at each meal
  • 3-4 meals per day + 1-2 snacks
  • Variety of family foods including meat, fish, eggs
  • Continue breastfeeding as often as child wants

2-5 Years

Family Diet

  • At least 1 full cup (250ml) at each meal
  • 3-4 meals per day + 1-2 snacks
  • Variety of family foods
  • If refuses new food, offer "tastes" several times
  • Talk with child during meals, keep eye contact

Key Messages

Healthy Eating Tips

  • Add one spoonful of extra oil/fat to child's food
  • Give thick uji made from cereals enriched with sugar, milk, groundnuts
  • Include meat, fish, vegetables, beans, eggs
  • Give milk and fruits daily
  • Do not mix more than two cereals
